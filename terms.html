Terms

BTC Helper is an educational research and planning tool. Use it only with your own judgment and risk limits.

No financial advice

BTC Helper is not financial advice, investment advice, tax advice, legal advice, or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any asset.

The daily stance and planner are general educational tools. They do not know your income, expenses, debt, taxes, custody setup, investment horizon, or risk tolerance.

Manual execution

BTC Helper does not place trades, connect to accounts, custody funds, or automate Bitcoin purchases.

Any purchase, sale, reserve decision, or account action is performed outside BTC Helper by the visitor.

Data limitations

BTC Helper uses public feeds and public chart/table review that may be delayed, incomplete, unavailable, revised, or interpreted incorrectly.

Source-health labels describe source completeness and freshness. They are not probabilities of future price movement.

No warranty

BTC Helper is provided as-is. There is no guarantee that the site, data, calculations, benchmark, documents, or planner will be accurate, available, complete, or suitable for any specific purpose.
