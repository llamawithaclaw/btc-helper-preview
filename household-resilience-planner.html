    Why these measures?

    The score is a transparent planning heuristic — not a credit score, financial plan, or Bitcoin return forecast.

    Show methodology

    The score rewards household conditions that make long-horizon accumulation more durable: positive margin, emergency liquidity, manageable debt pressure, survivable essential costs, and accumulation that does not consume all available slack.

    Cash-flow marginHigher monthly slack improves resilience because shocks are less likely to force borrowing or asset sales.
    Emergency bufferLiquid savings are treated as a base layer because the research links buffers to lower hardship risk.
    Debt pressureRequired debt payments reduce flexibility, especially when paired with thin buffers.
    Essential costsHousing, food, utilities, insurance, and irregular essentials can crowd out long-horizon planning.
    Medical exposureMedical and insurance costs can operate like a resilience premium that must be carried before savings can build.
    Accumulation durabilityThe planner favors accumulation that can survive stress without becoming forced selling, high-cost debt, or interrupted savings.
