Weekly Macro Thesis

A weekly macro backdrop for Bitcoin accumulation planning. It summarizes the current thesis, disagreement, and what to watch as conditions change without claiming engine validation.

How to read it

The weekly thesis is background context for the daily read. It explains macro pressure, disagreement, and what would matter next without changing the daily posture by itself, proving BTC Helper efficacy, or replacing dated evidence bundles.

What it is not

It is not a price target, trading signal, formal backtest, direct engine-validation record, or replacement for the private plan. Use Today for action and Track for the public record.
