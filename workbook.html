Your household · Budget workbook

Household budget workbook

The Household Planner as a spreadsheet you keep on your own computer: your budget, essential costs, cash reserves and what is left each month.

Download the workbook (.xlsx, 17 KB)

Your numbers stay with you. The workbook has no macros, no links to other files or websites and no data connections, and it sends nothing anywhere. If you open it in an online spreadsheet service, that service stores your copy.

What is inside

  • Start here: how to use it, in a few lines.
  • Inputs: income, essential costs, minimum debt payments, flexible spending, accessible cash and money already earmarked, each split into everyday lines. Enter 0 for a line that does not apply: a blank line means “not known yet”, never zero.
  • Calculations: the same arithmetic as the Household Planner, with every step visible. You cannot change it by accident.
  • Guidance: the same result wording as the planner, including which part of the safety floor decides.
  • Definitions: the planner's assumptions A1 to A3, word for word.

It works without any Bitcoin. It adds no rules, thresholds or advice of its own: if an entry is not a number, or is negative, it shows no result until that is fixed.

Which programs open it

  • Tested: LibreOffice Calc 24.2, in automated calculation tests (not interactive use, printing or non-US settings). Its results match the Household Planner in 445 test households.
  • Not tested: Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets. We make no claim that it works in them. Money amounts inside sentences are formatted in US dollars.
