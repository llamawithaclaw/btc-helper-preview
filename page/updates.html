Alert rules

Useful updates fire only when there is something to review, act on manually, or learn from. The weekly summary matters most while the daily posture remains stable.

Behavior-changing alertsImmediate alert

Send when the model posture changes behavior: review optional reserve, hold optional allocation, advanced baseline hold, or a source failure that invalidates the read.

Context changesBatched or weekly

Normal-to-Cautious style shifts and factor movement can matter, but they should not create urgent inbox noise when the baseline instruction is unchanged.

Source-health changesImmediate or batched alert

Send when source coverage materially improves, degrades, or recovers after a stale/degraded run.

Underlying conditions moveWeekly summary

Summarize valuation, holders, flows, macro, and leverage even when the top-line posture stays unchanged.

No-change daily readsNo email by default

Quiet days remain available by RSS/archive, but should not create inbox noise unless a reader chooses the full feed.

Plan reviewMonthly reminder

Nudge the reader to review their local plan and resilience floor without asking BTC Helper to store those values.
