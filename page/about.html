About

About

BTC Helper is an open-access guide to sound money, household resilience and Bitcoin, with the research behind it. Its Bitcoin part is a rule-based daily read and a planning tool.

What it does, and what it does not do

  • It explains how money holds value, how households can become more resilient, and what Bitcoin is and is not.
  • It offers free household tools that run in your browser, and a daily rule-based read of the Bitcoin market with its sources and limits.
  • It does not give individualised financial advice, execute trades, hold assets, connect to exchanges, sell trading signals, or predict short-term prices.

Who runs it, funding and conflicts

BTC Helper is an independent educational research project operated by Arne Kotte. Editorial, methodology and public-correction responsibility currently rests with Arne Kotte as operator.

BTC Helper does not provide individualized financial advice, execute trades, custody assets, connect to exchanges, sell trading signals, or receive exchange/referral compensation. There is no current brokerage, exchange, custody, paid referral, affiliate, signal-selling, or sponsor relationship behind the public product. If one is added later, it should be disclosed before it influences the site.

BTC Helper is Bitcoin-related research. The operator, contributors, or reviewers may hold Bitcoin or related assets. Treat the output as educational decision support, not an independent recommendation to buy, sell, or hold.

How the work is done

Much of this site, its software and its research drafts are written with the help of AI systems, including an AI agent and an AI assistant, under human editorial responsibility. Papers have not been through external peer review; their methods and limitations are stated with each paper.

Checking what we publish

Claims are kept narrow, every daily read is archived, weak or stale sources are shown rather than hidden, and the household tools keep your numbers in your browser. Source families and freshness are public; raw input values and the exact thresholds are not published.

  • Track record: the forward record of daily reads and their limits.
  • Data and sources: source families, freshness and coverage.
  • Why this reading?: how the daily read is made.
  • Evidence desk: historical cases and what they can and cannot show.
  • Corrections and security, privacy and terms.

Corrections and accessibility problems: support@btchelper.app. Security reports: security@btchelper.app. Please include the page address, the date and a short description of what looks wrong, and never send keys, passwords or account statements. Public reads are corrected in the public record, not by quiet rewrites. X is used for announcements, not as the correction record.
