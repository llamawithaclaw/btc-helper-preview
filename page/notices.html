Where the numbers and fonts on this site come from, and on what terms they are used.

Copyright

Market data used by the Bitcoin conditions read

The read uses inputs from third-party market-data providers. One value is shown today, as on the current site: the daily BTC reference price (with its change), in the daily read, its archive and the downloadable latest-read file. Whether it may continue to be shown is part of the open data-rights decision for its provider; its terms are unclear. The other inputs' values are not republished: for those the site shows only whether each was available, when it was observed and the band it fell in. A further value appears only once its provider's terms allow it, together with the credit that provider requires.

Naming a provider or agency as a source does not mean that it endorses this site.

This product uses the FRED® API but is not endorsed or certified by the Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis.

Statistics cited in the research

The research papers and essays contain our own charts and tables of public statistics. Figures and tables name their sources:

  • Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System: Survey of Household Economics and Decisionmaking (SHED) public-use data and data visualisations; Distributional Financial Accounts.
  • U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, including the Consumer Price Index and the Consumer Expenditure Surveys.
  • Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED), Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis.
  • U.S. Census Bureau, American Community Survey. Estimates derived from these tables are our own calculations.
  • Federal Housing Finance Agency, House Price Index.
  • Agency for Healthcare Research and Quality, Medical Expenditure Panel Survey (MEPS).
  • USDA Economic Research Service, Household Food Security in the United States.

Conclusions drawn from these data are the authors' own, not those of the agencies named.
