Weekly brief

A short read on the wider economic backdrop, published on Saturdays.

Interpretation, not a signal. The brief explains the backdrop and what to watch. It never changes the daily read, and it never changes your household result. It is not a price target or a trading signal.

How it is published

  • A new brief appears on Saturday mornings, US Eastern time, and is usually announced on X.
  • Each brief has its own dated address, /weekly-macro-thesis/<date>/, and stays available there. A brief can be corrected after it is published.
  • The archive page lists the latest brief first.
