This week in one sentence

The weekly macro thesis remains cautious: liquidity growth appears to be stalling, even while funding conditions remain orderly. Bitcoin scarcity does not establish a near-term price floor, and targeted market support is not the same as broad monetary easing.

What changed since last week

  • A high level of available liquidity can coexist with a weakening impulse for asset prices; direction matters as well as size.
  • The current macro discussion points to softer collateral support, not an established system-wide funding crisis.
  • Targeted equity support in China does not by itself confirm a broad monetary stimulus turn.
  • Renewed Bitcoin price weakness challenges early bottom calls without resolving the longer-term monetary thesis.

BTC Helper implications

  • Treat a durable recovery as a hypothesis requiring demand and funding confirmation, not as a consequence of fixed supply.
  • Watch whether policy support reaches broader credit and funding conditions before assuming it benefits Bitcoin demand.
  • Keep the ability to withstand forced selling separate from long-term conviction.
  • This weekly macro outlook provides context; BTC Helper’s mechanical daily signal remains separate and unchanged.

Qualitative figures

Counter-thesis

  • Orderly funding could cushion slowing liquidity growth and allow risk appetite to stabilize.
  • Broader easing could follow targeted support, improving the backdrop before cautious expectations adjust.
  • Bitcoin demand could recover without an immediate acceleration in global liquidity.

What would change it

  • Renewed liquidity growth alongside sustained Bitcoin demand would weaken the cautious recovery assessment.
  • Broader funding strain and persistent selling would strengthen the near-term downside-risk case.
  • A rebound without demand confirmation or broader easing would leave the recovery case unresolved.

Watch items

  • Whether liquidity growth resumes or continues to flatten.
  • Whether collateral softness spreads into funding stress.
  • Whether targeted support in China develops into broader monetary easing.
  • Whether Bitcoin rebounds are accompanied by sustained spot demand and reduced selling pressure.
  • Whether bond-market signals of weaker growth are confirmed by broader credit conditions.
