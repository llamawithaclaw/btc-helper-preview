Your household

Your household

Tools for checking your budget and cash buffer. They work without Bitcoin, and your numbers stay on your device.

The tools

Household Planner

A quick check, about five minutes. It works out your monthly room, your emergency buffer and a safety floor, and says which part decides the result.

In your browser. Nothing is saved unless you press Save.

Open the Household Planner

Budget workbook

The same check as a spreadsheet you keep on your own computer, with every step of the arithmetic visible.

.xlsx file. Tested in LibreOffice Calc only; not tested in Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets.

See the workbook

Detailed resilience check

A longer check: irregular bills, high-interest debt, protected cash and simple stress scenarios. It has an optional section for people who already hold Bitcoin.

In your browser. Saves only if you choose “Save locally”.

Open the detailed check

Which one should I use?

  • Start with the Household Planner if you want a clear answer quickly.
  • Use the workbook if you prefer a spreadsheet, or want to keep a copy offline.
  • Use the detailed check if your budget has irregular bills or debts you want to look at more closely.
  • Use the budget starter first if you don't know your totals yet: list your own income, costs and savings, then type its totals into the planner. It only adds up what you enter; it gives no result of its own.

The three use their own calculations; they are not combined into one score. My Plan, under Bitcoin today, is a different tool: a local plan for people who already follow a written Bitcoin buying schedule. It is not a household budget check.

Educational tools, not personalised financial advice. A result is only as good as the numbers you enter.
