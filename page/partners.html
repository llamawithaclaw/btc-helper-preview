Trust/Partner Overview
Inbound partner overview

BTC Helper for accumulation platforms

An explainable stance layer for recurring-buy, reserve-aware, and education-led Bitcoin accumulation workflows. This page is an inbound overview for interested teams, not an announcement of an active partnership program.

Ask about partner fit Review methodology
ExplainableAccumulation-focusedSource-health awareManual/user-executed
Partner problem

Recurring-buy products need more nuance than static DCA or dip heuristics.

Many accumulation products are either fixed-schedule DCA or simple price-trigger logic. BTC Helper is designed for a middle path: an explainable output contract that separates the scheduled baseline, optional reserve behavior, market context, and source health without pretending to know the future.

Product thesis
Better accumulation policy should be interpretable, operationally honest, and usable even when source health is imperfect.
What BTC Helper provides

A reserve-aware output contract with explicit source-health boundaries.

baseline_instructionContinue or hold the scheduled baseline according to the user's selected boundary.
reserve_instructionHold, deploy, or build optional reserve without confusing it with baseline behavior.
market_contextAggressive, Normal, Cautious, Pause, or Unclear as the explanatory market backdrop.
source_healthHigh, Moderate, Reduced, or Unclear as input freshness and completeness context.
Rationale

Plain-language explanation of the factor pressure behind the current read.

Coverage quality

Surfaces live, fallback, recently recovered, or degraded source conditions.

Evidence archive

Combines dated public reads with curated replay cases and transparent sample-size boundaries.

Integration concepts

Possible partner surfaces

Public-read widget

Display baseline instruction, reserve instruction, market context, source health, rationale, as-of time, and methodology version inside an existing education or recurring-buy surface.

Partner-core feed

A thinner partner-facing read feed designed for commercialization review without exposing proprietary model details.

Reserve-aware planner

Translate posture into manual or product-level accumulation workflows while keeping execution under user control.

Research review desk

Use methodology, public archive, and replay cases as product/research diligence inputs.

Evaluation path

An inbound evaluation can stay lightweight.

  • Define the partner use case: education, recurring-buy support, reserve logic, or internal research.
  • Agree on source requirements and production-grade provider expectations.
  • Compare partner-core outputs against the fuller research model over live reads and replay cases.
  • Review compliance, product language, and user-control boundaries before any integration.
Boundary
BTC Helper is not an exchange execution layer, not financial advice, and not a price-target engine. The public output is human-readable, but exact thresholds, weights, override rules, and calibration details remain private.
Evidence and diligence

Start with the public decision record.

The public surface shows current reads, archive history, methodology, benchmark treatment, degraded-day handling, and replay evidence. It is intentionally modest: record first, claims second.
