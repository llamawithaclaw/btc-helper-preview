Maturity labels

Maturity says how settled the document is. It does not say what topic it covers.

Established public methodologyCurrent public product or methodology explanation. It describes the operating framework, not proven efficacy.
Current public synthesisStable public synthesis or recurring commentary surface that may be updated as conditions change.
Research paperDeveloped public analysis with a stable conclusion, still separate from peer-reviewed publication.
Working paperDeveloped analysis that may still change as data, methods, or interpretation improve.
Measurement noteFocused note on variable construction, source quality, replication, or data limitations.
PrototypeExperimental tool or index that should not carry validated-score weight.
Supporting materialBackground, objections, or detail behind a clearer starting point.
Superseded / archivedPreserved for history, but not the preferred current methodology or starting point.

Evidence role

Evidence role answers what the document can actually support. No current public library item is labeled as direct engine efficacy validation.

Product methodExplains how BTC Helper is intended to work.
Validation method / governanceExplains evidence handling, source quality, or validation process without claiming performance.
Direct engine validationWould directly test BTC Helper decision efficacy; no current public library item is allowed to claim this.
Household evidenceDescribes household constraints that can affect whether an accumulation plan is durable.
Macro interpretationInterprets the macro backdrop and factor implications; it is not a model-validation result.
Bitcoin thesis contextInforms the broader Bitcoin/monetary argument without validating BTC Helper policy performance.
Measurement / methodsSupports reproducibility and data interpretation.
Hypothesis / exploratoryEarly or experimental work that should not carry major conclusions.
