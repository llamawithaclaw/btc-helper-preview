How to read it

Fresh means fresh for that source cadence, not a claim that the market signal is correct.

Source-health rubric

Source health is reproducibility context for the public read. The exact model weighting stays private, but the public components are stable enough for readers to understand why a read is clean, limited, or withheld from benchmark language.

Freshness

whether each source is current for its own expected cadence.

Coverage

whether required domains have enough public inputs to interpret the read.

Acquisition reliability

whether collection was direct, fallback, import-assisted, or unstable.

Transformation success

whether the public normalization step completed without exposing raw inputs.

Internal coherence

whether the source run had contradictions or warnings that limit usability.

Correction/revision state

whether a known correction, replacement, or timestamp review is attached to the run.

Substitution severity

whether missing inputs were replaced, softened, or withheld from benchmark language.

Evidence-bundle protocol

The latest public read stores a dated evidence bundle with source timing, frequency-aware freshness, correction status, normalized factor states, transformation version, and a content hash. The bundle ID and hash identify the serialized record; they do not prove source correctness, model accuracy, or future performance.
