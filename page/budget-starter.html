Your household · Budget starter

Budget starter

List your own income, costs and savings in your own words. See the monthly totals, and download the list for a spreadsheet.

Your list stays in this browser. No account, no upload: nothing you enter is sent anywhere. Nothing is saved unless you press “Save in this browser”, and you can erase it at any time. Leave an amount blank if you don’t know it yet: it is shown as unknown, never counted as zero.

This is a starting list, not advice. It only adds up what you enter. It gives no score, makes no judgement about your household, and never asks about Bitcoin.

1. Your list

Make categories that fit your life, such as “Home” or “Getting to work”, and add a line for each amount. Tag each line with one of the Household Planner’s six groups, so the totals match the planner’s boxes.

The examples are made-up households, not real data. Loading one replaces the list on screen; a list you saved stays saved until you press Save or Erase.

Your list is empty. Add a category to begin, or load a fictional example.

How weekly and yearly amounts become monthly. Weekly amounts are multiplied by 52 and divided by 12. Yearly amounts are divided by 12. Monthly amounts are used as entered. Each line is then rounded to the nearest cent (half a cent rounds up), and totals add the rounded lines.

Accessible cash and money already earmarked are amounts you hold now, so they are not converted. Earmarked money, such as taxes due or a planned purchase, is not emergency cash.

3. Use the totals in the Household Planner (optional)

The Household Planner does not read this list, and this page does not send it there. If you want its check, type these six totals into the planner yourself. Where a total is not known, leave the planner’s box blank or tick “I don’t know yet”: the planner then says what it still needs instead of guessing.

Open the Household Planner

The planner, not this list, applies its stated assumptions. Its safety floor has two parts, and it shows money for optional investing, in any asset, only when the floor is met, your buffer target is met and money is left each month. A market read, including BTC Helper’s, never overrides that.

4. Download your list

Downloads are made in your browser when you press a button. The CSV file opens in spreadsheet programs; the JSON file is a plain copy of everything, for other programs.

File options

  • The file is UTF-8 text with commas between columns. Amounts are plain numbers in dollars with two decimals, such as 1650.00 (the list accepts at most two decimal places); a blank amount stays an empty cell, marked “unknown (blank)”.
  • A name that starts with =, +, - or @ is written with an apostrophe in front, so a spreadsheet program shows it as text instead of running it as a formula.
  • The file name carries today’s date, for example budget-starter-2026-10-20.csv.

Which programs open the CSV file

  • Tested: LibreOffice Calc 24.2 (24.2.7.2), in automated tests that open exported CSV files (with and without the byte order mark) and convert them back. Every name came back unchanged, every amount came back with the same value, and no name became a formula, even with Calc’s “evaluate formulas” import option on. Not tested in interactive use or with non-US settings.
  • Not tested: Microsoft Excel and Google Sheets. We make no claim that the file works in them.

5. Keep it on this device (optional)

Saving keeps the list in this browser’s storage on this device only. Erase removes it and clears the list on screen.
