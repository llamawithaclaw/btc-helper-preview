How BTC Helper earns trust

BTC Helper should feel dependable because its claims are narrow, its daily reads are archived, source weakness is visible, and planner values stay local to the visitor browser.

  • The daily output separates baseline instruction, reserve instruction, market context, source health, and coverage.
  • The public record reports zero behavior-changing events when no behavior-changing events have occurred.
  • Provider/source families and freshness are public; raw inputs, thresholds, weights, calibration, and private validation notes remain outside the public surface.
  • Corrections and accessibility issues use support@btchelper.app; security reports use security@btchelper.app.
