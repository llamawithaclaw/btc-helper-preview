Learn · Sound money

What is sound money?

Money you can rely on over time: it holds its value reasonably well, its supply isn't changed on a whim, and it works for everyday life.

Sound money is an old idea with a simple question at its heart: can you count on your money tomorrow, and the year after?

ValueWe think dependable money and long-term thinking matter. That is our editorial starting point, not a research finding, and “sound” is a debated ideal rather than a certified category.

Seven qualities people look for

ValueThese are ideals, not a scientific classification.

  • Holds purchasing powerWhat a unit buys today it buys, roughly, in years to come.
  • Resists arbitrary dilutionNew units can't simply be created at will, so existing holders aren't quietly diluted.
  • DurableIt doesn't decay or wear out.
  • PortableIt's easy to carry or send.
  • DivisibleIt splits into small amounts for everyday prices.
  • VerifiablePeople can check that it's genuine.
  • Supports long-term thinkingWhen value is more predictable, planning and saving for the future may be easier.

Every money is a trade-off

ClaimNo money scores perfectly on all seven. Cash, bank deposits and gold each have strengths and weaknesses that depend on the country and institutions involved. Supply rules alone do not guarantee stable prices or usefulness, and some economists argue that a flexible money supply helps in a crisis. These are trade-offs to understand, not a ranking.

EvidenceThis page makes no claim about how any particular money has kept or lost value. Such claims need named sources (period, data and measurement); where our research makes them, the papers give their sources (Research: money and inflation).

From principles to habits

The same ideas can apply at home: living within your means, keeping reserves you can reach, and thinking in years.

How we label statements

  • ValueWhat we care about, or an editorial safety boundary, stated openly.
  • ClaimA statement that could be checked.
  • EvidenceWhat supports or challenges a claim, with sources, or a note saying plainly what is not yet sourced.
  • DecisionWhat you choose for your own situation. We never make it for you.
