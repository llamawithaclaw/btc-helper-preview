Contact

Email is the only way to reach us; there is no contact form. Optional email updates are a separate choice: see Updates, and Privacy for how that information is handled.

These addresses are unchanged from earlier versions of this site.

Start an email about

  • A data correction: the page or read date, and what looks wrong or out of date.
  • An accessibility problem: keyboard, screen reader, contrast, zoom or phone navigation.
  • A research question: methodology, evidence or the library.
  • Feedback on any page or tool.
  • A security or privacy concern.

Never send wallet keys, seed phrases, exchange logins, account statements, screenshots showing balances, or your Household Planner numbers. We will never ask for them.

Replies are best effort. X is used for announcements only.
